How it works...

‍

Juvéderm is a gel-based dermal filler. It's made up of hyaluronic acid, a naturally occurring sugar in our skin, that helps retain moisture and add volume. Juvéderm smoothes wrinkles and adds a volume boost to areas like the lips and cheeks. It works by injecting the gel under the skin, plumping and lifting to create a more youthful and refreshed appearance.

FAQs

Get answers to common questions about our Juvederm treatments

What's the difference between Juvederm and Botox?

While both treatments address signs of aging, Juvéderm filler adds volume and “fills” wrinkles using hyaluronic acid, while Botox temporarily relaxes muscles to reduce the appearance of lines.

What can I expect after my treatment?

First, a numbing cream will be applied to minimize any discomfort. Then the tiny injections will put the Juvéderm right where you want it! It's usually pretty quick, about 15 to 60 minutes, depending on the treatment area and amount of filler needed. It’s completely normal to feel some slight pinches or pressure. Afterwards, you can get right back with your day! You might have a bit of redness or swelling for a few days, but that's just your skin settling down.

Is Juvederm safe?

Juvederm is FDA-approved and considered safe when administered by  trained professional.If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, you will want to hold off on Juvéderm. We will talk about any medical history or medications during your consultation to make sure Juvéderm is a great fit for you.In general,  anyone that is generally healthy and is looking for a little volume, wrinkle-smoothing, and feature-boosting would be a great candidate!

Will it hurt?

Nope, not much at all! Most Juvéderm products contain lidocaine, a local anesthetic, making the pain very minimal. You may feel a few tiny pinches, but nothing major! Afterwards, you may experience some temporary side effects. Some experience swelling, redness, bruising, and minor discomfort. But these are generally reported to be minor and should subside within a few days!

What if I change my mind?

Most are thrilled with their results, but if necessary, we can inject the hyaluronidase enzyme to dissolve Juvéderm. From there, we can adjust your Juvéderm treatment plan to better meet your beauty goals!

How long will my results last?

Results generally last from 6 months to 2 years, depending on the specific type of Juvéderm. Touch-up sessions can help maintain your stunning results!

Is there downtime? Can I go back to work? 

One of the things that makes dermal fillers so great is there's little downtime. There's no reason you can't go back to work afterwards, although keep in mind that you may experience some redness, swelling or bruising.

When will I see my results?

You'll start to see results almost immediately, and can expect to see full results in about 2 weeks.
