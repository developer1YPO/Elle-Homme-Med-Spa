FAQs

Get answers to common questions about our wrinkle relaxation treatments

How long do results last?

Neurotoxin treatments typically last 3-4 months. Individual results may vary based on metabolism and muscle activity. We recommend follow-up treatments to maintain optimal results.

Are treatments painful?

Our experienced practitioners use fine needles and can apply topical numbing cream to minimize discomfort. Most clients describe the procedure as a quick pinch with minimal pain.

Who is a good candidate?

Adults seeking to reduce dynamic wrinkles and prevent future lines are ideal candidates. We conduct thorough consultations to ensure treatments match individual skin needs and health history.

What areas can be treated?

We commonly treat forehead lines, crow's feet, frown lines, and neck bands. Our precision approach allows targeted treatment of specific muscle groups for natural-looking results.

How quickly will I see results?

Initial changes appear within 3-5 days, with full effects visible after two weeks. The treatment progressively smooths wrinkles and prevents new line formation.
